What

What is universal design?

Design that works for as many people as possible, without special changes later.

On this page

Definition

  • Coined by architect Ron Mace and the Center for Universal Design at NC State.
  • Ability changes with age, injury, environment and device.
  • Chip link: Chip's show is built for the whole stadium, not one section.

The 7 principles

  • Equitable Use, Flexibility in Use, Simple and Intuitive Use, Perceptible Information, Tolerance for Error, Low Physical Effort, Size and Space for Approach and Use.
  • One Chip example for each. Perceptible Information: Chip uses signs and gestures.

Norman's design concepts

  • Affordance, feedback, visibility, mapping, constraints.
  • Inclusion Principle: send every message through more than one channel.

Web standards

  • HTML for structure, CSS for presentation, JavaScript for behavior.
  • Works across browsers and devices, loads faster, is easier to maintain.

Universal design, accessibility, inclusive design

  • How the three ideas overlap and where they differ.

Web accessibility basics

  • WCAG levels A, AA and AAA. AA is the usual target.
  • POUR: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust.

Who benefits

  • Auditory, cognitive, physical, speech and visual disabilities.
  • Chip link: a performer in a suit has limited vision and muffled hearing, a situational disability.
  • Assistive tech: screen readers, captions, magnifiers, keyboard and switch access, voice control.
